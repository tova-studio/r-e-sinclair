The workshop

Do you have a dyno for tuning?

Yes. Our workshop has a Dyno Dynamics 4WD dyno. A chassis dyno lets a car run under load inside the workshop, so tuning can be done and checked in controlled conditions.

Because it is a four-wheel-drive dyno, it suits a wide range of vehicles. Together with our large workshop, it lets us cater for any job, big or small. That covers everything from full engine rebuilds and fabrication to complete rewiring, installation and tuning of any aftermarket ECU.
